# Review Intent

## Intent
Decide whether the change satisfies the stated intent and is safe to keep.

## Inputs
- The active intent
- The changed files
- Relevant context files

## Outputs
- Findings only
- Each finding includes evidence and severity
- Each success criterion marked met, unmet, or untested

## Constraints
- Review only; do not modify files
- State what was not checked

## Stop when
Every criterion has a status and every finding has evidence.
