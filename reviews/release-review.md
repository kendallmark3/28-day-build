# Release Review Intent

## Intent
Decide whether IntentWorkbench is ready for a public demonstration and 28-day-course completion.

## Inputs
- `intent/project-intent.md`
- `reference-final/` or the learner's completed app
- `context/`
- evidence records

## Outputs
- Success criteria matrix: met / unmet / untested
- Blockers with direct evidence
- Non-blocking improvements
- Gap statement: what was not checked

## Constraints
- Review only
- No praise without evidence
- Do not invent defects

## Stop when
Every project success criterion has a status and every blocker has evidence.
