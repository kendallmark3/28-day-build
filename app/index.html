                Learn it. Teach it. Master it.

                The arc behind The Ultimate Guide to Claude by Mark Kendall: “learn how the system works, teach what you have learned, and through repetition master the underlying principles.”

                Learn

                Pick one task you repeat with AI. Write its intent with the four parts plus constraints and a stop condition, run the intent check, and run it. You end with one real intent file.

                Teach

                Give the intent to a teammate. Watch them use it, fix whatever confused them, and record the time saved. You end with a shared, reusable asset.

                Master

                Mastery comes from repetition. In the book’s words: “You do not master intent by reading about it. You master it by writing one, running it, watching what happens, and writing a better one.”

                About IntentWorkbench

                IntentWorkbench is a small tool for turning one-off AI work into something your team can keep. It runs in your browser and stores what you write in your browser’s local storage. It is built over 28 days, one written intent at a time. Start with intent. Add complexity only when the evidence demands it.
