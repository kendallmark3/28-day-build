# UX Standard

- **Quiet and clear:** at most one filled (primary) button is visible per view; one font family for text; body text has a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1.
- **Next action:** after every action that changes something, a status message says what happened; each view ends with a link to the next action.
- **Empty states:** every list or section that can be empty says what belongs there and what to do.
- **Progressive disclosure:** advanced controls (Jira story, reset) use the outlined secondary button style, never the filled one.
- **Keyboard focus:** every button, link, and field shows a focus outline of at least 2px when tabbed to.
- **Mobile:** at 375px wide there is no horizontal scroll, the first view's main button is in the window at load, and every button is at least 40px tall.
