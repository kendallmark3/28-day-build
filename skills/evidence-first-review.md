# Skill: Evidence-First Review

## Purpose
Review completed work against its intent without inheriting the builder's assumptions.

## Procedure
- Read the stated intent first.
- Inspect the resulting change.
- Mark every success criterion: met / unmet / untested.
- Report findings with direct evidence.
- Identify what was not checked.
- Challenge likely failure modes without inventing defects.

## Output
Structured findings plus a gap statement.

## Guardrail
Review only. Do not modify the work while reviewing it.

Project owner
